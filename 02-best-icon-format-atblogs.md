# Best Icon Format for ATblogs

If you are like me, a perfectionist or just cannot settle for less, you probably often spend time testing which image formats are supported by each platform. Here are the results for each Standard.site-compliant blog platforms in the ATmosphere network.

(in alphabetical order)

- **GreenGale 🍃**: use a high resolution `.png`, it will be resized and compressed; other image formats are converted to `image/png`
- **Leaflet 🍀**: `.webp` works perfectly; `.png` is a secondary option
- **Offprint 📖**: `.png` is the best; `.webp` is not yet supported
- **Pckt 👝**: you can't go wrong with `.webp`, of course `.png` is supported

> [!tip] Friendly reminder\
> Keep your blog icon as small as possible, ideally 200 KiB at most.

If you need a tool for optimizing images while keeping the quality high, I recommend [Squoosh](https://squoosh.app). It is pretty simple and self-explanatory.

While it is web-based, everything happens client-side or in your browser. Once you have an optimized blog icon, upload it to your Standard.site-compliant blog!

Enjoy!
