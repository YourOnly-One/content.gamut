# SNS, Social Media Terminologies Explained

Have you come across terms like Social Web, Fediverse, Threadiverse, Nostr, ATproto, ActivityPub, and DDFON? They can sound quite techie and confusing, let's demystify them!

---

Last update: 2026-10-25 [@291.67](https://beattime.live) (15:00 UTC+8)

---

(**in alphabetical order**)

<dl role="list" id="mycelial-web-definition-list">
  <dt role="listitem" id="dfn-activitypub">
    <dfn role="term"><p><b>ActivityPub (AP)</b></p></dfn>
  </dt>
  <dd id="dfn-activitypub-01">
    <div role="definition">

   The protocol behind the <a href="#dfn-fediverse" title="Fediverse">Fediverse</a> network, recommended by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C) since 2018-01-13.
    </div>
  </dd>

  <dt role="listitem" id="dfn-atmosphere">
    <dfn role="term"><p><b>ATmosphere</b></p></dfn>
  </dt>
  <dd id="dfn-atmosphere-01">
    <div role="definition">

   A friendly name for the entire network built on top of <a href="#dfn-atproto" title="ATproto">ATproto</a>. [^atproto-atmosphere-20230503] [^atproto-atmosphere-20230711] [^atproto-atmosphere-20240901-paul-frazee] Coined by Gordon Mohr ꧁🧿🧿꧂ in 2023-05-03 and by shreyan (unusual) in 2023-07-11.
   
   Also known by the name <a href="#dfn-bluesky" title="Bluesky">Bluesky</a>—based on the flagship <a href="#dfn-pds" title="PDS">PDS</a>—in other countries.
    </div>
  </dd>

  <dt role="listitem" id="dfn-atproto">
    <dfn role="term"><p><b>ATproto</b></p></dfn>
  </dt>
  <dd id="dfn-atproto-01">
    <div role="definition">

   The protocol that powers the <a href="#dfn-atmosphere" title="ATmosphere">ATmosphere</a> network, often incorrectly referred to as <a href="#dfn-bluesky" title="Bluesky">Bluesky</a>.
    </div>
  </dd>
  
  <dt role="listitem" id="dfn-blockchain">
    <dfn role="term"><p><b>Blockchain</b></p></dfn>
  </dt>
  <dd id="dfn-blockchain-01">
    <div role="definition">

   A cryptographic ledger, popularized by cryptocurrencies, and also used in <a href="#dfn-ddfon" title="DDFON">DDFON</a> <a href="#dfn-sns" title="SNS">SNS</a> like Audius (music), Hive (content), and Verasity (video).
    </div>
  </dd>

  <dt role="listitem" id="dfn-bluesky">
    <dfn role="term"><p><b>Bluesky</b></p></dfn>
  </dt>
  <dd id="dfn-bluesky-01">
    <div role="definition">

   The first public server powered by the <a href="#dfn-atproto" title="ATproto">ATproto</a> protocol. Bluesky is only one of many ATproto servers. (see also: <a href="#dfn-atmosphere" title="ATmosphere">ATmosphere</a>)
    </div>
  </dd>

  <dt role="listitem" id="dfn-ddfon">
    <dfn role="term"><p><b>DDFON</b></p></dfn>
  </dt>
  <dd id="dfn-ddfon-01">
    <div role="definition">

   Stands for &#8220;Decentralized, Distributed, Federated, Open Network&#8221;, a term I coined in 2011. [^ddfon-yohan-yukiya-01] [^ddfon-yohan-yukiya-02] <!-- also known as "<a href="#dfn-mycelial-web" title="mycelial web">mycelial web</a>". -->
    </div>
  </dd>

  <dt role="listitem" id="dfn-diaspora">
    <dfn role="term"><p><b>diaspora*</b></p></dfn>
  </dt>
  <dd id="dfn-diaspora-01">
    <div role="definition">

   The name of the protocol that powers <a href="#dfn-the-federation" title="The Federation">The Federation</a> network.
    </div>
  </dd>

  <dt role="listitem" id="dfn-did">
    <dfn role="term"><p><b>DID</b></p></dfn>
  </dt>
  <dd id="dfn-did-01">
    <div role="definition">

   The 「Decentralised IDentity」 is a permanent, digital serial number that is unique per user, it acts as a universal identifier that remains the same even if a user moves to a different service.
    </div>
  </dd>

  <dt role="listitem" id="dfn-fediverse">
    <dfn role="term"><p><b>Fediverse</b></p></dfn>
  </dt>
  <dd id="dfn-fediverse-01">
    <div role="definition">

   The friendly name for the network using the <a href="#dfn-activitypub" title="ActivityPub">ActivityPub</a> protocol and its predecessors (<a href="#dfn-ostatus" title="OStatus">OStatus</a> (2010–2020), <a href="#dfn-openmicroblogging" title="OpenMicroBlogging">OpenMicroBlogging</a> (2008–2010)). Coined by Mark Eckenwiler in 2012-05-25 and by Marjolein Katsma in 2013-01-24. [^coined-fediverse-mark-eckenwiler] [^coined-fediverse-marjolein-katsma] [^coined-fediverse-twitter]
    </div>
  </dd>

  <dt role="listitem" id="dfn-handle">
    <dfn role="term"><p><b>Handle</b></p></dfn>
  </dt>
  <dd id="dfn-handle-01">
    <div role="definition">

   A friendly username that serves as an easy-to-remember online nickname instead of a complex ID. In other networks, like the <a href="#dfn-atmosphere" title="ATmosphere">ATmosphere</a>, a handle can be updated anytime as an account is associated with its permanent <a href="#dfn-did" title="DID">DID</a>, not the handle.
    </div>
  </dd>

  <dt role="listitem" id="dfn-identiverse">
    <dfn role="term"><p><b>Identiverse</b></p></dfn>
  </dt>
  <dd id="dfn-identiverse-01">
    <div role="definition">

   The original name for the <a href="#dfn-fediverse" title="Fediverse">Fediverse</a> network, coined by Luke Slater in 2010-03-14. [^coined-identiverse-luke-slater] [^coined-identiverse-identica]
    </div>
  </dd>

  <dt role="listitem" id="dfn-jabber">
    <dfn role="term"><p><b>Jabber</b></p></dfn>
  </dt>
  <dd id="dfn-jabber-01">
    <div role="definition">

   A federated instant messaging network, the first <a href="#dfn-ddfon" title="DDFON">DDFON</a>. The name was dropped in favor of <a href="#dfn-xmpp" title="XMPP">XMPP</a>.
    </div>
  </dd>

  <dt role="listitem" id="dfn-mastodon">
    <dfn role="term"><p><b>Mastodon®</b></p></dfn>
  </dt>
  <dd id="dfn-mastodon-01">
    <div role="definition">

   A <a href="#dfn-fediverse" title="Fediverse">Fediverse</a> software using the <a href="#dfn-activitypub" title="ActivityPub">ActivityPub</a> protocol, incorrectly used to refer to the entire Fediverse network. It is a registered trademark that should only be used within the context of the Mastodon® software.
    </div>
  </dd>

  <dt role="listitem" id="dfn-matrix">
    <dfn role="term"><p><b>Matrix</b></p></dfn>
  </dt>
  <dd id="dfn-matrix-01">
    <div role="definition">

   An <abbr title="Internet Relay Chat">IRC</abbr>-like federated messaging platform similar to <a href="#dfn-xmpp" title="XMPP">XMPP</a>, serving as both the protocol and the network name.
    </div>
  </dd>

    <dfn role="term"><p><b>Mycelium</b> [network]</p></dfn>
  </dt>
  <dd id="dfn-mycelium-network-01">
    <div role="definition">

Originally suggested to replace the term <a href="#dfn-fediverse" title="Fediverse">Fediverse</a> but redefined in 2022.

    <dfn role="term"><p><b>Mycelial Web</b></p></dfn>
  </dt>
  <dd id="dfn-mycelial-web-01">
    <div role="definition">

   Multiple networks of fungal threads (mycelium) is called mycelia or mycelial. In tech, mycelial is the umbrella term for mycelium networks like <a href="#dfn-xmpp" title="XMPP">XMPP</a>, <a href="#dfn-fediverse" title="Fediverse">Fediverse</a>, <a href="#dfn-matrix" title="Matrix">Matrix</a>, <a href="#dfn-nostr" title="Nostr">Nostr</a>, and <a href="#dfn-atmosphere" title="ATmosphere">ATmosphere</a>; a friendly name for <a href="#dfn-ddfon" title="DDFON">DDFON</a>.
    </div>
  </dd>
  -->

  <dt role="listitem" id="dfn-nostr">
    <dfn role="term"><p><b>Nostr</b></p></dfn>
  </dt>
  <dd id="dfn-nostr-01">
    <div role="definition">

   It is a protocol that powers the <a href="#dfn-nostrich" title="NOSTRich">NOSTRich</a> network.
    </div>
  </dd>
  <dd id="dfn-nostr-02">
    <div role="definition">

   The name stands for 「Notes and Other Stuff Transmitted by Relays」.
    </div>
  </dd>

  <dt role="listitem" id="dfn-nostrich">
    <dfn role="term"><p><b>NOSTRich</b></p></dfn>
  </dt>
  <dd id="dfn-nostrich-01">
    <div role="definition">

   The friendly name of the network powered by the <a href="#dfn-nostr" title="Nostr">Nostr</a> protocol.
    </div>
  </dd>

  <dt role="listitem" id="dfn-openmicroblogging">
    <dfn role="term"><p><b>OpenMicroBlogging</b></p></dfn>
  </dt>
  <dd id="dfn-openmicrobloging-01">
    <div role="definition">

   The first protocol of the <a href="#dfn-fediverse" title="Fediverse">Fediverse</a> from 2008–2010.
    </div>
  </dd>

  <dt role="listitem" id="dfn-ostatus">
    <dfn role="term"><p><b>OStatus</b></p></dfn>
  </dt>
  <dd id="dfn-ostatus-01">
    <div role="definition">

   The <a href="#dfn-fediverse" title="Fediverse">Fediverse</a> protocol, active from 2010–2020. [^pleroma-federation-ostatus-dropped]
    </div>
  </dd>

  <dt role="listitem" id="dfn-pds">
    <dfn role="term"><p><b>PDS</b></p></dfn>
  </dt>
  <dd id="dfn-pds-01">
    <div role="definition">

   「Personal Digital Store」, a system for capturing a lifetime of personal documents and media. First described by Bell and Gemmell for the **MyLifeBits project** at Microsoft Research. [^coined-pds-01]
    </div>
  </dd>
  <dd id="dfn-pds-02">
    <div role="definition">

   「Personal Data Store」, a citizen-empowering personal data infrastructure—defined by Mydex in their September 2010 white paper [^coined-pds-02], and established as a global standard by the World Economic Forum [^coined-pds-03]. **Project Danube** was a reference implementation of a PDS while also federated to the <a href="#dfn-fediverse" title="Fediverse">Fediverse</a> network (2010–2013). [^coined-pds-04]
    </div>
  </dd>
  <dd id="dfn-pds-03">
    <div role="definition">

   「Personal Data Server」, is a modern implementation of a PDS specific for the <a href="#dfn-atproto" title="ATproto">ATproto</a> protocol.
    </div>
  </dd>

  <dt role="listitem" id="dfn-secure-scuttlebutt">
    <dfn role="term"><p><b>Secure Scuttlebutt (SSB)</b></p></dfn>
  </dt>
  <dd id="dfn-secure-scuttlebutt-01">
    <div role="definition">

   A cryptographic network where content is shared only with followers.
    </div>
  </dd>

  <dt role="listitem" id="dfn-semantic-web">
    <dfn role="term"><p><b>Semantic Web</b></p></dfn>
  </dt>
  <dd id="dfn-semantic-web01">
    <div role="definition">

   One of the original meanings of <a href="#dfn-web3-point-O" title="Web3.0">Web3.0</a>, where the web becomes 「capable of analyzing all the data on the Web—the content, links, and transactions between people and computers」 through W3C standards.
    </div>
  </dd>

  <dt role="listitem" id="dfn-sns">
    <dfn role="term"><p><b>SNS (Social Networking Service)</b></p></dfn>
  </dt>
  <dd id="dfn-sns-01">
    <div role="definition">

   The original term for <a href="#dfn-social-media" title="social media">social media</a>, still commonly used in Korea and the Pacific-Asia region.
    </div>
  </dd>

  <dt role="listitem" id="dfn-social-media">
    <dfn role="term"><p><b>Social Media</b></p></dfn>
  </dt>
  <dd id="dfn-social-media-01">
    <div role="definition">

   The term for <a href="#dfn-sns" title="SNS">SNS</a> in English-speaking countries.
    </div>
  </dd>

  <dt role="listitem" id="dfn-social-web">
    <dfn role="term"><p><b>Social Web</b></p></dfn>
  </dt>
  <dd id="dfn-social-web-01">
    <div role="definition">

   Also known as <a href="#dfn-web3" title="Web3">Web3</a> or <a href="#dfn-web3-point-O" title="Web3.0">Web3.0</a>, it promotes an open, interoperable web instead of closed silos. It is about building platforms that interoperate through federation and bridges, while ensuring resiliency through distribution and decentralization. [^web3-jesse-stay] [^web3-loic-le-meur-01] [^web3-loic-le-meur-02] [^web3-loic-le-meur-03] It is the same as 「Open Social Web」 and 「Open Social」, and is a very broad term that goes beyond <a href="#dfn-ddfon" title="DDFON">DDFON</a>. [^socialweb-wikipedeia] [^socialweb-w3c-01] [^socialweb-w3c-02] [^socialweb-w3c-03]
    </div>
  </dd>

  <dt role="listitem" id="dfn-the-federation">
    <dfn role="term"><p><b>The Federation</b></p></dfn>
  </dt>
  <dd id="dfn-the-federation-01">
    <div role="definition">

   The name of the network running on the <a href="#dfn-diaspora" title="diaspora*">diaspora*</a> protocol.
    </div>
  </dd>

  <dt role="listitem" id="dfn-threadiverse">
    <dfn role="term"><p><b>Threadiverse</b></p></dfn>
  </dt>
  <dd id="dfn-threadiverse-01">
    <div role="definition">

   Refers to threaded <a href="#dfn-fediverse" title="Fediverse">Fediverse</a> platforms like Lemmy, Kbin, and Mbin.
    </div>
  </dd>
  <dd id="dfn-threadiverse-02">
    <div role="definition">

   In Korea, it refers specifically to Meta's Threads platform with <a href="#dfn-fediverse" title="Fediverse">Fediverse</a> integration. [^threadiverse-02-korea]
    </div>
  </dd>

  <dt role="listitem" id="dfn-web3-point-O">
    <dfn role="term"><p><b>Web3.0</b></p></dfn>
  </dt>
  <dd id="dfn-web3-point-O-01">
    <div role="definition">

   Refers to <a href="#dfn-semantic-web" title="Semantic Web">Semantic Web</a> as coined by Sir Timothy John Berners-Lee in 2006. [^web3-point-O]
    </div>
  </dd>
  <dd id="dfn-web3-point-0-02">
    <div role="definition">

   Also refers to the <a href="#dfn-social-web" title="social web">social web</a> **Le Web3** conference in France in 2007 and earlier. [^web3-loic-le-meur-01] [^web3-loic-le-meur-02] [^web3-loic-le-meur-03]
    </div>
  </dd>
  <dd id="dfn-web3-point-0-03">
    <div role="definition">

   It was also later used to refer to <a href="#dfn-blockchain" title="blockchain">blockchain</a> technology [^web3-tim-berners-lee-w3c] as defined by Gavin Wood in 2014. [^web3-blockchain-wikipedia]
    </div>
  </dd>

  <dt role="listitem" id="dfn-web3">
    <dfn role="term"><p><b>Web3</b></p></dfn>
  </dt>
  <dd id="dfn-web3-01">
    <div role="definition">

   Initially coined for the <a href="#dfn-social-web" title="social web">social web</a> conference in 2007 by Loic Le Meur. [^web3-loic-le-meur-01][^web3-loic-le-meur-02][^web3-loic-le-meur-03]
    </div>
  </dd>
  <dd id="dfn-web3-01">
    <div role="definition">

   It was also later used for <a href="#dfn-blockchain" title="blockchain">blockchain</a> technology [^web3-tim-berners-lee-w3c] as defined by Gavin Wood in 2014. [^web3-blockchain-wikipedia]
    </div>
  </dd>

  <dt role="listitem" id="dfn-xmpp">
    <dfn role="term"><p><b>XMPP (eXtensible Messaging and Presence Protocol)</b></p></dfn>
  </dt>
  <dd id="dfn-xmpp-01">
    <div role="definition">

   The first federated instant messaging platform, originally named <a href="#dfn-jabber" title="Jabber">Jabber</a>.
    </div>
  </dd>
</dl>
